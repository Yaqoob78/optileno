# Optileno: go-to-market (first 30 days)

*Goal: 30 fixed-price freelancers using Optileno on a real project, and a clear yes/no on the kill criteria in [STRATEGY.md](STRATEGY.md).*

## Positioning in one line

**Every small change has a price. Charge it, gift it, never lose it.**

Always lead with the *pain moment* ("Can we just add a pricing page?"). Don't lead with the feature list. Everyone who has freelanced has a story; the product's job is to make them tell it.

## Where the first 30 come from

| Channel | Why it fits | What to post |
|---|---|---|
| Designer and developer communities (r/freelance, r/web_design, r/graphic_design, r/editors, r/webdev; Designer News; Indie Hackers) | The pain is a constant topic there | A story post ("I added up every 'quick tweak' on my last 5 projects: $2,400"), then the calculator. Link the product only when someone asks. Follow each community's self-promo rules. |
| X/Twitter and Threads, design and indie-maker circles | Short, relatable, screenshot-friendly | The "anatomy of a quick tweak" chat as an image or GIF; the client page as a before/after |
| LinkedIn (freelancers with 1–10k followers) | Freelancers who sell to businesses live here | "The one line I send when a client asks for extras" plus the warm reply template |
| Freelancer newsletters and YouTubers (design business, video-editing business) | Borrowed trust | Offer a free "scope page" template walkthrough; no paid sponsorship yet |
| Your own network (the founder freelances) | Warm, honest feedback | Use it on your own projects first; screenshots from real use beat mockups |

## Free tools that bring people in (SEO + shares)

Each is a small public page that ends in "do this automatically in Optileno":

1. **Scope-creep cost calculator** (already on the landing page; give it its own URL)
2. **Revision policy generator**: pick rounds, get a clause for your proposal
3. **"Not included" list templates** per discipline (website, logo, video, writing)
4. **Change-order email templates**: warm, brief, and last-round versions

Target searches: *scope creep freelancer*, *how to charge for extra revisions*, *change order template freelance*, *revision policy template*, *client asking for more work than agreed*.

## Week by week

| Week | Do | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Use Optileno on your own live project. Screenshot your real client page. Post two story posts. DM 20 freelancers you know. | 10 people have tried the demo; 3 set up a real project |
| 2 | Give the calculator its own page. Post the "quick tweak" story in 3 communities. Hold 5 short calls with people who logged a request. | 15 real projects; list of misread requests collected |
| 3 | Fix the top misreads in the rule set. Publish the revision-policy generator. Ask active users for a one-line quote about money recovered (real names only, with permission). | 25 real projects; first real testimonial |
| 4 | Product Hunt launch (maker story + demo GIF). Measure against the kill criteria. | Decision: proceed to Pro, rework capture, or revisit |

## Rules

- **No fabricated social proof.** No invented testimonials, ratings, or user counts. Use real quotes with permission, or none.
- **Don't promise features that don't exist.** Pro is labeled "planned" for a reason.
- **Talk to every early user.** The best features will come from the requests Optileno misreads.
